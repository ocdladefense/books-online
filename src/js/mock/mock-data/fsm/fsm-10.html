Felony Sentencing Guidelines

Except for the rules in OAR 213-017, 213-018, 213-019, and 213-021, which provide the seriousness rankings for ranked felonies, the first nine chapters of this manual set out the Felony Sentencing Guidelines rules in their current texts.

Chapter 10 of the first four editions of this manual contained the complete texts of the guidelines rules as they existed at the time of each manual’s publication. Because of the frequency of rule amendments (which first stated in 1993), and what has become a difficult process of keeping accurate account of guidelines amendments, like the fifth edition, this edition’s Chapter 10 does not contain the complete texts of all rules. Instead, Chapter 10 includes only the current guidelines grid (referenced in OAR 213-004-0001(2), and which rule is set out in Chapter 1).1

For the user’s convenience, the current rules in their complete texts are available from the State Archives section of the Secretary of State’s web page. Its URL is https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/ruleSearch.action, and it may be accessed by clicking here. Once there, use the drop-down menu to find “213 - Criminal Justice Commission.” Click on it to be taken to the page containing the Felony Sentencing Guidelines.

The rules that are not included in the first nine chapters of this manual are those found in divisions 017, 018, 019, and 021 of Chapter 213. Those four divisions are explained below.

• OAR 213-017 CRIME SERIOUSNESS SCALE

This division provides the Felony Sentencing Guidelines’ complete Crime Seriousness Scale. Offenses are listed by ORS citation, crime title, and felony classification. When an offense has been subcategorized, it appears in two or more crime categories. An abbreviated description of the subcategorized factors is included in division 017 for each subcategory of the offense as it appears on the Crime Seriousness Scale. Divisions 018, 019, and 021 should be consulted for complete descriptions of offense subcategories.

• OAR 213-018 OFFENSE SUBCATEGORIES

Offenses in this division are divided into different subcategories of the guidelines’ Crime Seriousness Scale. The definition of each offense in this division relies on a subclassification factor, which captures a wide spectrum of criminal conduct. Offense subcategories have been identified to classify the most common forms of these offenses. Consequently, each subcategory includes a unique set of characteristics that represents a different degree of crime seriousness for sentencing purposes. The subcategorized offenses are presented in alphabetical order.

• OAR 213-019 OFFENSE SUBCATEGORIES FOR NON-CANNABIS
DRUG-RELATED OFFENSES

This division provides crime seriousness subcategories for non-cannabis drug-related offenses.

• OAR 213-021 OFFENSE SUBCATEGORIES FOR CANNABIS OFFENSES

This division provides crime seriousness subcategories for cannabis-related offenses.

  1. Chapter 1 explains that the guidelines first took effect on November 1, 1989, and apply to felonies committed on or after that date. See, e.g., OAR 213-020-0001. With extremely limited exceptions, guidelines amendments apply only to crimes committed on or after the amendments’ specified effective dates. It is important that defense counsel ensure that courts use the applicable versions of the guidelines in their clients’ sentencings.↩︎
